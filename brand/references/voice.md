# Diffusion Studio — voice guide

Defines the words, claims, and recurring voice assets. Apply it to titles, labels, scripts,
captions, calls to action, and voiceovers.

## Writing

- Lead with the result or action.
- Use short words, short sentences, and active voice.
- Cut any word that adds no meaning.
- State what the product does. Do not add hype or an unsupported claim.
- Keep product names and interface labels exact.
- Avoid familiar figures of speech.
- Rewrite copy that breaks a length cap. Do not shrink the type.

## Tone

Sound clear, calm, and sure. Let the work supply the energy. Do not sound grand, cute, vague,
or eager to impress.

## Voiceovers

Reuse an approved recording from `assets/audio/voiceovers/` when its words and delivery fit.
Do not reuse a recording only because it is available. Store recurring intros, taglines, and
calls to action; keep one-off narration with its project.

PLACEHOLDER — there is no approved house voice, delivery guide, or recurring recording yet.
Do not invent one. Name the missing choice when a request depends on it.
